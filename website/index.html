DEVIL'S DOOR

REACH THE DOOR. TRUST NOTHING.

An atmospheric dark fantasy silhouette ninja action-platformer. Master acrobatic double jumps, fluid katana dash-slashes, shuriken throwing, and battle through 10 deceptive campaign levels to conquer the Demonic Oni Gate.

01 / PHILOSOPHY & ART

Dark Fantasy Silhouette Benchmark

Your objective is deceptively simple: reach the ancient Runic Shrine to ascend. The journey is fraught with deceptive rope bridges, hidden spike pits, swinging pendulum battleaxes, and patrolling Shadow Ninjas.

🥷

Flowing Cloth Scarf Physics

Athletic black silhouette ninja with a conical kasa hat, dual katanas on back, glowing cyan eye slit, and a dynamic 7-node Verlet cloth red scarf that ripples with wind and velocity.

⚔️

Katana Slashes & Shurikens

Execute golden blade dash-slashes through multiple enemies, somersault double-flips, wall slides with sparks, and ranged shuriken star throws.

🌀

Cinematic Exit Ascension

Reach the ancient Torii Obelisk Shrine at the end of each level. Watch the vertical celestial light beam erupt as the ninja sheathes their blade and ascends into the cosmic vortex.

02 / CAMPAIGN ACTS

4 Biomes, 10 Epic Levels

Experience escalating difficulty, dynamic deceptions, and tactical enemy encounters across 4 distinct atmospheric biomes.

ACT I Mountain Shrines (Lv 1 - 3)

Twilight mountain ridges, the famous rope bridge collapse deception, emerald bamboo groves, and the Cavern of Claws with swinging pendulum battleaxes.

ACT II Ancient Crypts (Lv 4 - 6)

Glacial ice caverns with hanging stalactite icicles, forgotten stone ruins with vertical wall-jump shafts, and venomous crimson thorn forests.

ACT III Shadow Stronghold (Lv 7 - 9)

Golden desert dunes with burning campfires, burning multi-tier pagoda roofs, and the deadly Hall of 1000 Blades with synchronized pendulum traps.

ACT IV Demonic Oni Gate (Lv 10)

The ultimate climax: Battle the giant Demonic Oni Samurai Boss with blue armor, dual giant katanas, ground shockwaves, and lightning storms to unlock the true Devil's Door!

03 / MOBILE ERGONOMICS

100% Touch Responsive Controls

Engineered with multi-touch precision for mobile and tablet browsers:

◀ ▶

Stylized Triangular D-Pad

Generous touch areas on bottom-left for comfortable thumb steering with instant response and zero input delay.

✦ ⚔️ ⬆️

Dedicated Action Cluster

Large tactile buttons on bottom-right for JUMP, SLASH, and SHURIKEN. Supports simultaneous multi-touch combinations (e.g. sprint + jump + slash).

📱

Safe Area & Gesture Lock

Fully locked against accidental browser scrolling or pinch-to-zoom (`touch-action: none`) with safe-area padding for modern notched displays.
