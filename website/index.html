DEVIL'S DOOR

ENDLESS SHADOW ASCENSION

An infinite dark fantasy ninja action-platformer. Run, jump, dash-slash, and survive through continuous procedural chunks as the world seamlessly morphs every 3 minutes across 5 atmospheric biomes.

01 / INFINITE PROCEDURAL ENGINE

Master the Endless Descent

Procedural terrain generation, glowing diamonds, swinging crescent battleaxes, and lethal shadow guards test your shinobi reflexes.

♾️

Infinite Procedural Chunks

No two runs are identical. High ledges, collapsing rope bridges, wall-jump shafts, and spike pits spawn dynamically ahead of you.

⏱️

3-Minute Biome Cycles

Every 180 seconds, the atmospheric sky, parallax background ridges, weather particles, and platform textures seamlessly morph in real time.

💎

Diamonds & High Scores

Collect glowing celestial diamonds, rack up distance multipliers, and cement your all-time distance record on the local leaderboard.

🪓

Lethal Demonic Traps

Dodge massive swinging pendulum blades, giant spinning crimson skull-saw gears, ceiling spikes, and reaching demon claws.

02 / FIVE SIGNATURE REALMS

Seamless Atmospheric Morphing

Every 3 minutes, the world transitions across the authentic dark fantasy visual aesthetics from the Archive collection.

REALM 01 Sunset Fortress Torii Gates & Campfires

Amber-crimson twilight skies, distant Torii gates, multi-tier pagodas, warm lantern halos, and floating golden embers.

REALM 02 Frozen Abyss Snow Ledges & Frost Trees

Glacial ice blue skies, snow-capped rocky cliffs, frosted dead pine branches, demon mask pillars, and drifting snow flurries.

REALM 03 Whispering Bamboo Swinging Crescent Blades

Deep emerald teal mist, dense vertical bamboo stalks, swinging pendulum battleaxes, and swirling teal bamboo leaves.

REALM 04 Demonic Thorn Crypts Skull-Saw Gear Wheels

Lilac purple twilight, giant spinning red skull-saw wheels, crimson thorny tentacles, and glowing demonic eyes in the fog.

03 / PRECISION SHINOBI ARSENAL

Master the Blade & Shuriken

⚔️

Katana Dash-Slash

Cleave through dual-blade shadow scouts and armored naginata spearmen with high-speed golden blade dash-slashes.

✦

THE DEMONIC ONI

Every 1,000 meters. One demon. One test of mastery.

Face the giant Demonic Oni Samurai Boss with ground shockwaves, dual katanas, and lightning strikes as you push your distance record to legendary heights.
