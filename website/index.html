02 / FOUR REALMS OF DECEPTION

10 Campaign Levels across 4 Biomes

Ascend through distinct atmospheric realms with unique hazards and visual aesthetics.

ACT I Mountain Shrines Levels 1 – 3

Sunset mountain ledges, the collapsing rope bridge deception, emerald bamboo groves, and the Cavern of Claws.

ACT II Ancient Crypts Levels 4 – 6

Glacial ice caverns with hanging stalactites, forgotten stone sanctums with wall-jump shafts, and venomous crimson thorn forests.

ACT III Shadow Stronghold Levels 7 – 9

Golden desert dunes with campfires, multi-tier burning pagodas, and the lethal Hall of 1000 Blades with synchronized pendulum traps.

ACT IV Demonic Oni Gate Level 10 • Final Battle

Face the giant Demonic Oni Samurai Boss with blue armor, dual katanas, ground shockwaves, and lightning storms to conquer the Final Gate.

03 / PRECISION CONTROLS

Master the Blade

Engineered for instant response on both desktop keyboards and mobile multi-touch screens.

⌨️

Desktop Keyboard

A / D or Arrow Keys to Move · Space / W to Jump · J / Z to Dash-Slash · K / X to Throw Shuriken · R to Restart.

📱

Mobile Touch Controller

Stylized triangular D-Pad on bottom-left, dedicated large Jump, Slash, and Shuriken circular action cluster on bottom-right with multi-touch precision.
