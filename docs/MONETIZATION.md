# MONETIZATION.md — Ethical Monetization Strategy

> **PROJECT**: DEVIL'S DOOR  
> **TAGLINE**: REACH THE DOOR. TRUST NOTHING.

---

## 1. Ethical Foundations

Devil's Door rejects exploitative mechanics, forced video interruptions, pay-to-win barriers, and artificial stamina timers.

### Core Non-Negotiables:
- **Zero Paywalls on Core Progression**: All 100-200 levels in the main campaign are 100% free and completable with skill and observation.
- **No Forced Interstitials on Retry**: Retries are instantaneous. Forcing an ad on death destroys the core learning loop and is prohibited.
- **Purely Optional Value**: Monetization respects the player's time and agency.

---

## 2. Monetization Pillars

### Pillar 1: Optional Rewarded Assist / Clues
- Players who are stuck on a difficult deception after $N$ failed attempts ($N \ge 8$) can optionally watch a short rewarded video to reveal a subtle visual hint (e.g. glowing footprint path).
- Completely voluntary.

### Pillar 2: "Remove Ads" Supporter Pass
- A one-time micro-purchase ($1.99 - $2.99) that removes all optional ad triggers and unlocks the "Founder's Supporter Glow" cosmetic aura.

### Pillar 3: Purely Cosmetic Customizations
- Alternate Player Core Auras (Spectral Gold, Neon Crimson, Holographic Violet).
- Custom Door Vortex Themes (Cosmic Portal, Dark Nebula).
- Zero stats or gameplay advantages.

### Pillar 4: Future Community & Challenge Packs
- Optional standalone expansion packs (e.g., *The Abyss Trials*, *Speedrun Gauntlet*) created in collaboration with verified community creators.
