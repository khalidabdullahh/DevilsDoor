## 1. Visual Philosophy & Benchmark

```
ATMOSPHERIC · SILHOUETTE · JAPANESE DARK FANTASY · READABLE
```

Devil's Door v2.0 blends high-contrast ninja silhouette character art with rich painterly parallax backdrops inspired directly by the 16 reference screens in `Archive/`:
1. **High Contrast Readability**: Shadow character silhouettes, bright glowing eye visors, and crimson particle cues are instantly readable against multi-stop twilight gradients.
2. **Painterly Layered Depth**: Multi-stop atmospheric skies, far parallax mountain ridges, mid-ground pagoda castles/bamboo groves/frosted winter trees, and reflective wet ground sheens.
3. **Restraint & Atmosphere**: Dynamic weather particles (golden embers, gentle snow flurries, bamboo leaves, purple spores) create immersion without cluttering gameplay readability.

## 3. The 16 Archive Scene Biomes (3-Minute Dynamic Cycles)

### Scene Family 1 — Sunset Fortress & Torii Gate (`images (4).jpeg`)
- **Sky**: Multi-stop gradient `#1c1032` $\to$ `#3b1238` $\to$ `#9f1239` $\to$ `#dc2626` $\to$ `#f59e0b`.
- **Celestial Body**: Radiant golden solar orb with volumetric atmospheric halo.
- **Parallax**: Distant mountain peaks, Torii gates, multi-tier pagoda castles.
- **Foreground**: Wet dark stone platforms with crimson water reflection sheen.

### Scene Family 2 — Frozen Snow Abyss & Oni Pillars (`images (3, 5, 7, 9).jpeg`, `media_1788408797536.jpg`)
- **Sky**: Glacial gradient `#061320` $\to$ `#0f2942` $\to$ `#1e486b` $\to$ `#60a5fa`.
- **Terrain**: Thick snow blankets, dripping icicles, dead bare winter trees, and carved Oni demon pillars.
- **Props**: Embedded red spears and katanas marking ancient battlefield ruins.

### Scene Family 3 — Whispering Bamboo Forest (`images (2, 8, 14).jpeg`)
- **Sky**: Emerald mist `#022119` $\to$ `#064e3b` $\to$ `#047857` $\to$ `#10b981`.
- **Terrain**: Dense vertical segmented bamboo stalks, high rope bridges, and ziplines.
- **Hazards**: Climbing red spiky demon tentacles and swinging crescent battleaxes.

### Scene Family 4 — Demonic Thorn Crypts & Skull Saw Gears (`images (6, 10, 17).jpeg`)
- **Sky**: Lilac twilight `#12071a` $\to$ `#2c103e` $\to$ `#581c87` $\to$ `#9333ea`.
- **Terrain**: Background Japanese castle tiled rooftops and wooden watchtowers.
- **Hazards**: Giant spinning crimson skull-saw gears with jagged teeth.

### Scene Family 5 — Sky Waterfall Chasms (`images (1, 16).jpeg`)
- **Sky**: Turquoise atmosphere `#032322` $\to$ `#0f4f4b` $\to$ `#0d9488` $\to$ `#2dd4bf`.
- **Terrain**: Towering vertical waterfall cascades, rising mist droplets, and wooden spike barricades.

### Scene Family 6 — Ancient Temple Ruins & Glowing Shrines (`images (11, 12, 13).jpeg`)
- **Sky**: Dark earthen tone `#1c1917` $\to$ `#292524` $\to$ `#44403c` $\to$ `#78716c`.
- **Props**: Glowing cyan obelisk shrines, pushable rune blocks, hanging yellow lanterns on branches, earthen vases, and rivers with sunken chests.
