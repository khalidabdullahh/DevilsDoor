## Roadmap Phases

- [x] **Phase 0 — Foundation & Architecture**
  - Repository structure, open-source governance suite, CI pipeline.
  - Complete documentation specifications in `docs/`.
  - Architecture Decision Records logged in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

- [x] **Phase 1 — Movement Sandbox & Physics Core**
  - Responsive character controller with coyote time (100ms) and jump buffering (120ms).
  - Deterministic AABB collision resolution with slopes and moving surfaces.
  - Camera tracking with subtle screen shake and boundaries.

- [x] **Phase 2 — Deception Engine Core**
  - Trigger $\to$ Condition $\to$ Action data-driven framework.
  - Core actions: platform shifting, collision toggling, hazard spawner, gaze pulse.

- [x] **Phase 3 — 2.5D Visual Pipeline & Lighting**
  - Depth-extruded isometric geometry and ambient drop shadows.
  - Controlled color family systems (Discovery Slate, Shadow Void).
  - Bespoke particle effects for player aura, death disintegration, and door vortex.

- [x] **Phase 4 — Cinematic In-Game Intro**
  - 7–10s atmospheric sequence ("FROM THE CREATORS OF AUREX" $\to$ "TRUST NOTHING").
  - Seamless transition to Level 1 with instant skip option.

- [x] **Phase 5 — 5-Level Handcrafted Prototype**
  - Level 1: *The First Assumption* (Collapsing bridge reveal).
  - Level 2: *Patience and Peril* (Velocity-sensitive trap & idle disclosure).
  - Level 3: *The False Exit* (Decoy portal dissipation & lower sanctum reveal).
  - Level 4: *Phase Shift* (Jump-triggered platform polarity inversion).
  - Level 5: *Gaze of the Shadow Devil* (Stealth pulse & atmospheric freeze).

- [x] **Phase 6 — Mobile Controls & Responsive UI**
  - Touch D-pad & Jump buttons with zero drift across mobile viewports.
  - Top HUD overlay with death counter, level title, and instant restart.

- [x] **Phase 7 — Official Landing Website**
  - Mobile-first, responsive marketing website with system-aware Light/Dark mode tokens.
  - Direct playable launcher integration.

- [ ] **Phase 8 — Content Expansion (Levels 6–25)**
  - Expand Acts I, II, and III with additional deceptive mechanics.

- [ ] **Phase 9 — Monetization & Telemetry Backend**
  - Integration of privacy-first analytics and ethical supporter cosmetics.

- [ ] **Phase 10 — QA & Beta Testing**
  - Cross-browser and multi-device automated testing and performance profiling.

- [ ] **Phase 11 — Official Trailer & Marketing Release**
  - In-engine recorded trailer highlighting real gameplay moments.

- [ ] **Phase 12 — Global Release & 100-Level Scale**
  - Long-term campaign rollout across web, mobile stores, and desktop platforms.
