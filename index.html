DEVIL'S DOOR

REACH THE DOOR. TRUST NOTHING.

A dark, atmospheric 3D ninja action-platformer where every death teaches something. Master precision katana combat, agile platforming, and navigate a deceptive 3D world of shifting ruins, crumbling bridges, and false portals.

PLAY NOW ➔ VIEW GITHUB ⚡
01 / PHILOSOPHY

Reach The Door. Trust Nothing.

Your objective is deceptively simple: touch the door to complete the room. The challenge is that the world, routes, hazards, rules, and even the Door will challenge your assumptions.

🥷

Ninja Movement & Combat

Responsive sprint, variable jump height, air control, and fluid katana strikes with knockback and hit sparks against patrolling Shadow Sentries.

💡

Every Death Teaches

Zero arbitrary punishment. Deceptions are discoverable, learnable, and fair. The ideal reaction after failure is: "Ah. Now I understand what happened."

🏛️

3D Dark Fantasy World

Powered by Babylon.js with real-time WebGPU/WebGL rendering, volumetric fog, dynamic shadows, flickering lanterns, and floating cherry blossom particles.

02 / DECEPTION ENGINE

The World Subverts Expectations

Powered by a data-driven Trigger-Condition-Action framework, the realm reacts dynamically to your decisions.

🌉

Collapsing Bridges & Crypts

Obvious high stone spans crumble into the abyss upon your step, safely revealing hidden subterranean crypts and ancient pathways.

🌪️

Decoy & Shifting Doors

Reaching an apparent Door may cause it to dissolve into shadow particles, unlocking genuine subterranean chambers or alternate dimensions.

⚔️

Shadow Sentry Combat

Engage armored ninja sentries with telegraph warnings, vision cones, and fluid swordplay as you fight toward the genuine exit.

03 / ACTION & ATMOSPHERE

Master the Blade, Question the Path

Cinematic side-focused 3D camera with depth framing and responsive katana combat.

"The obvious path is not always the correct path. Trust your blade, but doubt the world."

Level 01: The First Assumption serves as the production vertical slice, establishing the dark fantasy visual benchmark, responsive platforming flow, and signature deceptive architecture.
