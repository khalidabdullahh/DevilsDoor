# Project Governance

## 1. Overview & Principles

**Devil's Door** is an open-source, community-accessible project operated under a **Founder-Led Governance** model.

Our core model is defined as:
> **OPEN DEVELOPMENT + OPEN CONTRIBUTION + CURATED MERGING + FOUNDER FINAL AUTHORITY**

The purpose of this governance model is to ensure high technical velocity, protect the game's unique artistic and creative identity, safeguard against plagiarism, and maintain long-term architectural integrity.

---

## 2. Roles & Authority

### Founder & Lead Maintainer
- **Individual**: [Khalid Abdullah](https://github.com/khalidabdullahh)
- **Authority**:
  - Sets project vision, game mechanics, and thematic direction.
  - Final decision-maker on architecture, level approval, and release scheduling.
  - Retains exclusive administration over production secrets, official deployments, domain names, and official brand assets.
  - Manages maintainer permissions and repository privileges.

### Contributors & Community Members
- **Privileges**:
  - May fork the repository freely under the open-source license.
  - Submit issues, bug reports, performance improvements, and documentation fixes.
  - Propose new mechanics, levels, visual shaders, and accessibility enhancements via Pull Requests.
  - Participate in community design discussions.
- **Boundaries**:
  - Contribution does not automatically confer repository write permissions or product ownership.
  - Contributions are merged only after code review and alignment with the game design vision.

---

## 3. Decision-Making Process

1. **RFCs & Feature Proposals**: Community members may open an issue titled `[Proposal] <Feature>` describing mechanics or improvements.
2. **Review & Discussion**: Maintainers review the proposal against [`docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md`](docs/MASTER_CONTEXT.md) and [`docs/LEVEL_DESIGN_BIBLE.md`](docs/LEVEL_DESIGN_BIBLE.md).
3. **Founder Approval**: Major mechanical shifts, branding updates, or monetization integrations require explicit approval by Khalid Abdullah.
4. **Architectural Records**: Major architectural decisions are documented in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](docs/DECISIONS.md).

---

## 4. Intellectual Property & Commercial Rights Protection

1. **Dual-License Framework**: The source code is freely licensed under the MIT License for educational, community, and developmental collaboration. However, the game's commercial trademarks ("Devil's Door", "Aurex"), 4K scenery assets, character roster (#01 to #06), audio compositions, and narrative universe are the exclusive proprietary property of **Khalid Abdullah**.
2. **Plagiarism & Rebranding Protection**: No third-party entity or individual may re-upload, re-publish, or commercially monetize the proprietary artwork, character models, or trademarked title without explicit written licensing agreement signed by Khalid Abdullah.
3. **Monetization & Commercial Exploitation**: Commercial distribution across web portals (Poki, CrazyGames), mobile app stores (Google Play, App Store), desktop distribution platforms (Steam, Itch.io), and direct sponsorships remains exclusively under the authority of Khalid Abdullah.
